# Images Folder

Place your images here! They will appear randomly throughout the experience.

## Supported formats:
- JPG/JPEG
- PNG
- GIF
- WebP

## Naming:
Name your files sequentially:
- `1.jpg` (or `.png`, `.gif`, etc.)
- `2.jpg`
- `3.jpg`
- etc.

Or update the image list in `src/components/HomePage.jsx` around line 22 to match your filenames.

## Recommendations:
- Use 8-15 images for best variety
- Images will appear upside down when the letter is showing
- They appear in corners and edges of the screen
